# Content and microcopy

KONFRM is Arabic-first, using natural Egyptian/Modern Arabic suitable for the role and moment.

- Prefer short, specific action labels and human status language; never expose raw English enums or database/provider terminology.
- State the problem and recovery path in errors; avoid vague “something went wrong.”
- Do not imply urgency without a real product rule.
- Use emoji only as deliberate content decoration, never as the primary action/status system.
- Do not repeat branding next to the logo without a purpose.

Customer tone is reassuring and simple. Owner tone is clear and operational. Admin tone is precise and efficient. One role’s technical language must not leak into another role’s product surface.
