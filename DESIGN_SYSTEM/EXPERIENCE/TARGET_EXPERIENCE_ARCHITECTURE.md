# Target experience architecture — RECOMMENDED / FOUNDER REVIEW

## Customer

Persistent discovery/trips/account orientation; property and booking decision flows are contextual. Real imagery, dates, price and reassurance lead. Payment/chat only appear when canonical booking state permits them.

## Owner

Entry validates identity, then loads current-account operational data. Home leads with work waiting for action; Bookings, Properties, Messages and Wallet serve frequent operations. Calendar, account, verification and disputes remain contextual unless evidence changes their frequency.

## Admin

A validated desktop operational shell leads to queues and review context. Overview routes to actual work; search/filter/table/detail patterns support decisions. No full-screen decorative splash.

## Migration guardrail

Each future slice names its user job, current pain, target experience, Design System contracts, unchanged business rules, Founder acceptance steps and legacy exceptions removed. It must not generalize one role’s layout to the other two.
