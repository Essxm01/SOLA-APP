# Role UX principles

## Customer

Prioritize discovery, confidence, visual/price clarity, booking confidence, reassurance and easy recovery. Customer may use more real property imagery and discovery patterns, but uses the same foundations and never sees internal commission or owner-wallet detail.

## Owner

Prioritize control, operational clarity, status visibility, financial clarity, actionability and confidence that data is current. Avoid dense enterprise dashboards, dark finance terminals, card walls and tiny-text grids. Financial information is understandable without exposing unnecessary internal complexity.

## Admin

Prioritize decision speed, review context, queue management, search/filter/sort and auditability. Admin may be denser and table-oriented on desktop, but never creates a separate visual language.
